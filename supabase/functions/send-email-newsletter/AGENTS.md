# send-email-newsletter rules
- The daily email's What's on block takes rows from `get_topic_whats_on` (same rules as the feed) and filters them only through the pure `pickDailyEvents` in `dailyEvents.ts` (tested); one rule set keeps the email and the feed agreeing on what's still on.
- The weekly email's "The week ahead" uses the same rows filtered by `pickBriefingEvents` in `_shared/briefingEvents.ts` (shared with the feed's weekly briefing page); a send with no stories and no events is skipped, never sent empty.
- Events-focused emails (events first) show picture-led cards from `emailEvents.ts`: stored listing photo via the email image proxy, else the category illustration PNG (`eventArt.ts`, re-render from `EventCategoryIllustration` if the art changes) on the feed's `eventIllustrationTint`; news-led emails keep the compact list.
- A pinned story leads the weekly email while its pin lasts and the daily email once per pin (`pinLeadsEmail`; `topics.pinned_daily_led_at` is stamped only after a real daily send), so a long pin never repeats at the top of every daily.
