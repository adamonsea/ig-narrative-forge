# Event card rules
- Event descriptions on reader cards and social assets go through `completeDescription()` (`supabase/functions/_shared/eventDescription.ts`: whole sentences up to a limit, else whole word + ellipsis, never rewritten); owner edits set `events.description_edited` and `scrape-whats-on` keeps that text on every gather; one rule keeps every surface complete and owner wording safe.
- Event social assets come only from `src/components/events/social/`: a 9:16 poster with just the listing photo (else category art in the feed colour), a calendar-page date tile and the event name, laid out by `posterLayout.ts` inside Instagram's safe zones (tested); 5s motion + still from the same frame drawer; the caption (`eventCaption.ts`) carries every other fact and is editable before copy. Opened from the Desk event drawer's Social pack; news social exports stay separate, and a future automation hand-off reuses these files.
- Pinned events: owners pin live events one click at a time (`EventPinButton`, up to 9, pin lasts until the event passes) via guarded `set_event_pin`; readers see them as a fanned card deck above the feed (`PinnedEventsDeck`, vendored `src/components/card-pack/`) from `get_topic_pinned_events`; tapping jumps to the event's card, else its listing. Story pinning stays single.
