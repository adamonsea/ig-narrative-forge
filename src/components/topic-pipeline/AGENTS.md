# Desk rules
- The Desk's Live now list takes membership and order from `splitLiveNow()` in `src/lib/liveNow.ts` (same rules and display-date order as the public feed); written-but-not-live stories are split by `groupHeld()` into amber "needs your OK" (fresh drafts nothing will publish on its own), "Queued" (ready or scheduled) and the rest, so Live now matches what readers see and the owner sees what's waiting on them.
- Publishing from the Desk goes through the pipeline's `publishNow`: the story joins Live now at once via `withJustPublished()` (same feed rules) and stays faded with "Adding picture" until `illustrateOnPublish` finishes; the owner sees the result of their OK immediately, in its true feed position.
- A waiting story that repeats a live one is matched by `findLiveRepeat()` (`src/lib/liveNow.ts`); tapping the card or its "Duplicate live" tag opens `DuplicateCompareDrawer` (WorkspaceDrawer, both stories side by side, Skip / Publish too), so the owner decides from both versions, not a label.
- Story pool Publish/Skip respond at once: the card plays `flow-out` (toward Live now) or `skip-out`, then is moved or hidden optimistically while the save runs, and is put back if the save fails or the owner taps Undo; the Event pool is unchanged.
- Every story card (Story pool, needs your OK, Queued, Written not live, Live now) has a quick spike via `SpikeButton` (small muted ×, first in the action row with a gap before the main button, tooltip "Skip" / "Remove from feed"), wired to `archiveWithUndo` so it plays `skip-out` and offers Undo; small, apart and undoable keeps it fast without accidental spikes.
- Pinning: a Live now card has a small one-click `PinButton` at the end of its action row (24h pin, or unpin when pinned; up to 9 pinned at once), kept apart from the spike since they're opposing actions; the story drawer keeps the length picker (24 hours / 2 days / 7 days, or Unpin). All go through `useSetStoryPin`; the pinned card shows "Pinned · Xh left" and sits first via `withPinnedFirst`, matching the public feed.
- Story card Publish is `PublishSplitButton` (Publish + small ▾ menu with "Publish without picture"), counting as one action in the two-action budget; without-picture goes through `publishNowUpdate()` (`src/lib/publishStory.ts`, sets `skip_auto_illustration`) or the writer's `generateIllustration=false`, so story-illustrator blocks every later automated picture. New card variants go in that menu, never as extra buttons. Awaiting review cards on scheduled feeds read "Add to queue" / "Add to queue without picture" (`approveArticleLabels()`), and the job carries `owner_approved` so queue-processor never sends an owner-chosen story back for a second OK; it slots into the next free release time behind queued stories (queue-processor calls drip-feed-scheduler as soon as a story is saved ready), and on scheduled feeds "being written" rows show in Queued, not Live now.
- The Story pool reads top to bottom as three shelves: amber "needs your OK", "Queued · N" (written, going out on schedule) and "Awaiting review · N" (gathered articles Autopilot didn't write, via `ShelfLabel` with its tools menu; each card carries one quiet tag from `holdReasonTag()` in `src/lib/holdReason.ts` saying why, e.g. "No place named" or "73% · needs 75%", none when Autopilot is off); supporting folds (Written, not in the feed; Published) sit below, so every list has a name and finished stories never look like raw arrivals.
- The Desk Story pool / Live now lists load through `useMultiTenantTopicPipeline`: independent lookups run in parallel, cards render before slide text (fetched in the background, `slides_pending`), and the last-seen lists per feed come from `src/lib/deskCache.ts` (memory + same-tab, per account, cleared on sign-out) while every visit still refreshes from the server; returning to the Desk is instant without ever being the source of truth.
