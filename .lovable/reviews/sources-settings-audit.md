# Sources and Settings: what breaks the Workspace rules

## Sources
1. Add source was a long inline form: helper lines ("Works with any website…"), emoji (🔓 ⚠️ ✅), a validate button, frequency field, Title Case. **Now a 2-step drawer.**
2. "Find sources" opened its own pop-up with emoji, green palette colours, cheering lines ("All done! Your feed is ready."). **Now a quiet step in the same drawer.**
3. "New feed?" banner: raw blue colours, bold lead-in, long sentence.
4. Backfill pop-up: Title Case ("Backfill Historical Content"), explanation line, helper under the select.
5. Toasts: Title Case ("Content Gathering Complete"), emoji (⏸️), jargon ("arrivals queue").
6. Panels: sublines under "Trust everything from here" and Auto-publish; labels "Shown to readers as" / "Link we read".
7. Empty shelves: long sentences ("Paste a what's-on page above to add one").

## Settings (by area)
1. Fold titles are lists of contents ("More options: app icon, picture colours, …"); section headings use the serif display face.
2. Sublines under options and fields across Autopilot, Automation, Drip feed, News values, Categories, Look for, Clean up, Brand, Welcome card/About, Branding, Insight cards, Donations, AI assistants, Widget.
3. Small text (text-xs / 10–11px) in the same places plus shared help tooltips.
4. Title Case labels and buttons (Welcome Card, About Page, Save Branding, Donation Settings, Tier Name…).
5. Emoji in Insight card names (📈 👥 🎮 🧠 📅 🗂️).
6. Raw palette colours in Subscribers (blue/purple/emerald/amber) and Branding presets.
7. Icon-only buttons without tooltips (remove logo, remove term, dismiss, copy, revoke, add place…).
8. Several navy primary buttons on one page (Save branding, Save settings, Save donations, Classify stories…).
9. Welcome-card controls still show while reader welcome extras are paused.
10. AI assistant setup uses a pop-up instead of the side drawer.
